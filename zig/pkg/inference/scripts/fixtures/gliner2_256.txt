On Tuesday, the City of Riverton announced a public transit pilot with Acme Robotics and Northstar Analytics. Mayor Elena Ortiz said the program will add electric shuttles between the downtown library, the university medical center, and Harbor Station. The $4.8 million contract begins in September and runs for eighteen months. Acme will provide vehicles, while Northstar will analyze ridership data and publish monthly reports. Residents can submit comments through the Riverton Mobility Office before August 30. The project is funded by a state clean transportation grant and local capital improvements budget.
