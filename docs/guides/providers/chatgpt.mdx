---
title: ChatGPT Plan
description: Use a personal ChatGPT plan as the generator in a locally hosted Antfly
---

The `chatgpt` provider connects a personal ChatGPT plan to a locally hosted
Antfly. You authorize it from Antfarm or the CLI; Antfly keeps the OAuth
credentials and selects the plan's models for chat and retrieval answers.
Connecting a plan grants no database permissions.

## What It Provides

| Component | Inputs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Generator | Text | Local standalone deployments |

## Limits

- Image inputs are not supported.
- `api_url`, `max_tokens`, `max_completion_tokens`, and `rate_limit` cannot be set; the plan governs usage.
- A ChatGPT generator cannot be part of a fallback chain or retry policy, so a request never moves to a billed provider without your choice.
- Connections can be disabled with the `chatgpt` setting in the server configuration.
