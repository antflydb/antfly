# Antenna step 1 pilot, 2026-09-25

The first distillation pilot for Antenna (zig/pkg/inference/models/antenna/ANTENNA.md):
a ModernBERT-base student with GLiNER2.5 boundary heads, trained natively on
resident Metal. Result: **the student collapses to constant outputs, in the
native trainer and in upstream PyTorch alike.** The gate (within about 2 points
of gliner2.5-base) is not met. The collapse is not specific to ModernBERT: raw
DeBERTa-v3-base under the same fresh heads collapses the same way (see
Diagnosis). The recipe (fresh GLiNER heads, 4,560 rows, about 1,100 optimizer
steps) cannot teach GLiNER's label matching to an encoder that has not been
GLiNER-trained.

## Setup

- Data: `scripts/antenna/teacher_targets.py`, 4,560 train and 239 validation
  rows from the train splits of Banking77, AG News (classification, soft
  targets `0.5 * gold + 0.5 * sigmoid(Decide logit)` over 8–24 sampled labels)
  and CrossNER AI/literature/music, MIT Restaurant (gold entity spans). Rows
  over 128 upstream words are dropped.
- Students (`scripts/antenna/init_student.py`): `answerdotai/ModernBERT-base`
  at `8949b909ec900327062f0ebf497f51aef5e6f0c8` with (v1) fresh published
  boundary heads or (v2) gliner2.5-base's trained heads (`--heads-from`; both
  768 wide). 158.8M parameters.
- Native job: `antfly-inference finetune train gliner25`, resident Metal,
  full fine-tune, 2 epochs, batch 4 x accumulation 2, encoder lr 3e-5, task
  lr 5e-4, warmup 0.1, seed 2509, ReleaseFast, on a Mac Studio (M4 Max,
  36 GiB). 2,280 microbatches in 49.5 min.
- Controls (`scripts/antenna/upstream_control.py`): upstream GLiNER2's own
  trainer, same rows (hard labels), same learning rates, epochs and effective
  batch, FP32 on MPS.
- Evaluation: `scripts/antenna/baselines.py`, the step-0 harness and seeded
  subsamples (see 2026-09-25-baselines.md).

## Results

| Model (mean, in-domain / held-out) | Classification accuracy | NER F1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gliner2.5-base, released | 0.728 / 0.483 | 0.543 / 0.515 |
| gliner2.5-base + pilot recipe, upstream trainer | 0.808 / 0.467 | 0.723 / 0.624 |
| ModernBERT student v1, untrained | 0.152 / 0.137 | 0.010 / 0.005 |
| ModernBERT student v2 (trained heads), untrained | 0.152 / 0.181 | 0.011 / 0.007 |
| v1, native Metal | 0.133 / 0.145 | 0.000 / 0.000 |
| v1, upstream trainer | 0.116 / 0.140 | 0.000 / 0.000 |
| v2, native Metal | 0.134 / 0.146 | 0.000 / 0.000 |

Readings:

- On gliner2.5-base (whose encoder is already GLiNER-trained) the recipe lifts
  in-domain classification by 8 points and NER F1 by 18, and held-out NER F1
  by 11.
- The ModernBERT students collapse. Every classification label receives
  nearly the same logit (about -2.2 to -2.4) regardless of the text, and no
  entity is predicted even at threshold 0.05. The native classification loss
  ends at 0.368, the level of a constant all-negative predictor.
- The native trainer is not the cause: upstream's trainer collapses the same
  way. Trained heads do not rescue it (v2).

## Diagnosis

Short instrumented runs of upstream's model and losses (300 optimizer steps,
batch 4 x accumulation 2, AdamW, linear warmup 10%, clip 1.0, MPS on the
Studio; not a committed script). Every 25 steps a probe encodes six fixed texts
under one six-label classification schema and measures the fraction of the
`[L]` marker states' energy that varies with the text ("text variance"), the
mean pairwise cosine of text word states ("word cosine"), and argmax accuracy
of the classifier on the six texts.

| Run (300 steps) | [L] text variance, step 1 → 25 → 300 | Probe accuracy at 300 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gliner2.5-base, its own heads | 0.115 → 0.106 → 0.107 (at 100) | 0.83 |
| gliner2.5-base encoder, fresh heads | 0.115 → 0.162 → 0.161 (at 100) | 0.83 |
| raw DeBERTa-v3-base, fresh heads | 0.101 → 0.006 → 0.003 (at 100) | 0.17 |
| ModernBERT student, fresh heads | 0.100 → 0.023 → 0.008 | 0.17 |
| ModernBERT, classification rows only | 0.098 → 0.114 → 0.002 | 0.17 |
| ModernBERT, encoder frozen for 100 steps | 0.098 (frozen) → 0.013 | 0.33 |
| ModernBERT, encoder lr 5e-6 | 0.098 → 0.035 → 0.028 | 0.33 |
| ModernBERT, final norm scaled to DeBERTa's (0.37) | 0.101 → 0.015 → 0.021 | 0.17 |
| ModernBERT + cosine feature KD to gliner2.5-base | 0.100 → 0.020 → 0.011 | 0.17 |
| same, batch-centered KD | 0.100 → 0.029 → 0.014 | 0.00 |
| pure KD (no task loss), markers and words weighted equally | 0.099 → 0.007 → 0.006 | n/a |

(DeBERTa runs stopped near step 110 on an MPS allocation limit; their values
are at step 100.)

- The collapse is general to raw encoders under this recipe: raw DeBERTa-v3
  collapses faster than ModernBERT. What protects gliner2.5-base is its
  GLiNER-trained encoder, not the DeBERTa architecture.
- It happens in the first 25–50 steps and is not driven by loss magnitude:
  classification alone (encoder gradient norm about 12) collapses as fast as
  the full boundary loss (about 35,000). Freezing the encoder while the heads
  warm up, a lower encoder learning rate, and rescaling features delay or
  soften it but do not prevent it.
- With transplanted trained heads, ModernBERT's first-batch loss is about 230x
  gliner2.5-base's (pair loss 6,824 vs 8, soft-IoU 6,539 vs 91): the heads are
  confidently wrong on ModernBERT features. This is why v2's first loss was
  high; it is not the collapse's cause (fresh heads collapse too).
- Feature distillation from gliner2.5-base (student and teacher align one to
  one on words and schema markers; all 2,400 rows aligned) lowers the task
  loss (22 vs 90–280) but does not stop the collapse. The teacher's word states
  share a large common direction (word cosine 0.815); cosine distillation first
  aligns every student token with it (student word cosine 0.60 → 0.98 within
  25 steps), which erases the per-token residual that carries the text. At a
  marker distance of 0.08 the residual is still unmatched.

Next candidates:

1. Relational distillation: match the teacher's token-to-token similarity
   structure (marker-to-word and word-to-word cosine matrices) rather than
   absolute directions, which penalizes exactly the collapse measured here.
2. More data and steps: GLiNER2's own encoder training used a far larger
   corpus. Test whether the plateau breaks with a larger teacher-labeled set
   before building more machinery.
3. The earlier ModernBERT-specific leads (no [CLS] attention sink, start-of-text
   per-word tokenization) remain possible contributors but are not the primary
   cause, since raw DeBERTa collapses without them.

## Operational notes

- ModernBERT-base jobs need job budgets above the small-DeBERTa defaults:
  about 12 GiB backend (the largest batch needed 9.5 GiB resident at batch 4),
  6 GiB host (9 GiB to restore a checkpoint), and larger tape limits.
- Each run writes a 2.5 GB optimizer checkpoint plus a 0.6 GB export; the
  job's disk preflight reserves both.
- On a shared machine the live-memory admission keeps a 9.66 GB dynamic
  reserve; other training jobs blocked the laptop, so the pilot ran on the
  Studio.
