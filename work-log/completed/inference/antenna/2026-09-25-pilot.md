# Antenna step 1 pilot, 2026-09-25

The first distillation pilot for Antenna (zig/pkg/inference/models/antenna/ANTENNA.md):
a ModernBERT-base student with GLiNER2.5 boundary heads, trained natively on
resident Metal. Result: **the student collapses to constant outputs, in the
native trainer and in upstream PyTorch alike**, and the gate (within about 2
points of gliner2.5-base) is not met. The collapse is not specific to
ModernBERT: raw DeBERTa-v3-base under the same fresh heads collapses too. Any
freshly initialized component on top of the encoder (task heads or a
distillation projection) makes a constant encoder output the fastest early
descent, and 4,560 rows are too few to climb back out.

The follow-up diagnosis (below) found a recipe that works: **feature
distillation from gliner2.5-base's encoder over a large unlabeled-text pool,
through a projection fitted in closed form before training.** After 8,000
steps the ModernBERT student, read through gliner2.5-base's own heads, reaches
0.639 in-domain and 0.370 held-out classification accuracy (gliner2.5-base:
0.728 and 0.483), up from 0.13 collapsed. A second stage with half the pool
under entity schemas (still no labels) brings NER to 0.449 in-domain and 0.364
held-out F1 (gliner2.5-base: 0.543 and 0.515), and classification to 0.662
and 0.378. About two hours of Studio time in total.

## Setup

- Data: `scripts/antenna/teacher_targets.py`, 4,560 train and 239 validation
  rows from the train splits of Banking77, AG News (classification, soft
  targets `0.5 * gold + 0.5 * sigmoid(Decide logit)` over 8–24 sampled labels)
  and CrossNER AI/literature/music, MIT Restaurant (gold entity spans). Rows
  over 128 upstream words are dropped.
- Students (`scripts/antenna/init_student.py`): `answerdotai/ModernBERT-base`
  at `8949b909ec900327062f0ebf497f51aef5e6f0c8` with (v1) fresh published
  boundary heads or (v2) gliner2.5-base's trained heads (`--heads-from`; both
  768 wide). 158.8M parameters.
- Native job: `antfly-inference finetune train gliner25`, resident Metal,
  full fine-tune, 2 epochs, batch 4 x accumulation 2, encoder lr 3e-5, task
  lr 5e-4, warmup 0.1, seed 2509, ReleaseFast, on a Mac Studio (M4 Max,
  36 GiB). 2,280 microbatches in 49.5 min.
- Controls (`scripts/antenna/upstream_control.py`): upstream GLiNER2's own
  trainer, same rows (hard labels), same learning rates, epochs and effective
  batch, FP32 on MPS.
- Evaluation: `scripts/antenna/baselines.py`, the step-0 harness and seeded
  subsamples (see 2026-09-25-baselines.md).

| ModernBERT student v1, untrained | 0.152 / 0.137 | 0.010 / 0.005 |
| ModernBERT student v2 (trained heads), untrained | 0.152 / 0.181 | 0.011 / 0.007 |

| v1, upstream trainer | 0.116 / 0.140 | 0.000 / 0.000 |

Readings:

- On gliner2.5-base (whose encoder is already GLiNER-trained) the recipe lifts
  in-domain classification by 8 points and NER F1 by 18, and held-out NER F1
  by 11.
- The ModernBERT students collapse. Every classification label receives
  nearly the same logit (about -2.2 to -2.4) regardless of the text, and no
  entity is predicted even at threshold 0.05. The native classification loss
  ends at 0.368, the level of a constant all-negative predictor.
- The native trainer is not the cause: upstream's trainer collapses the same
  way. Trained heads do not rescue it (v2).

## Diagnosis

Instrumented runs of upstream's model and losses on the Studio (MPS; batch 4
x accumulation 2, AdamW, linear warmup 10%, clip 1.0; the harness is not
committed, it lives in `~/antenna-pilot/probe` there). Every 25 steps a probe
encodes six fixed texts under one six-label classification schema and
measures:

- **text variance**: the fraction of the `[L]` marker states' energy that
  varies with the text (gliner2.5-base: 0.115);
- **word cosine**: mean pairwise cosine of text word states (gliner2.5-base:
  0.815);
- probe accuracy of the classifier on the six texts.

Short runs (300 steps, the 4,560 pilot rows):

Readings:

- The collapse is general to raw encoders: raw DeBERTa-v3 collapses faster
  than ModernBERT. What protects gliner2.5-base is its GLiNER-trained encoder,
  not the DeBERTa architecture.
- It happens in the first 25–50 steps, whatever the loss magnitude
  (classification alone, encoder gradient norm about 12, collapses as fast as
  the full boundary loss at about 35,000), optimizer (AdamW or SGD) or
  learning rate (3e-6 still collapses).
- One mechanism explains every failure: whatever sits on top of the encoder
  starts random (task heads, or a distillation projection). Its output is
  noise, and the fastest way to cut that noise is a constant encoder output,
  which leaves the label prior or the teacher's mean. Pure regression through
  a random projection collapses the encoder completely (word cosine 0.998);
  the same regression through a projection fitted by ridge regression before
  any encoder update (R² 0.54–0.60) does not (word cosine 0.84).
- Cosine distillation without a projection collapses for a related reason:
  the teacher's states share a large common direction, and aligning every
  student token with it is the cheapest first step.
- With transplanted trained heads, ModernBERT's first-batch loss is about 230x
  gliner2.5-base's (pair loss 6,824 vs 8): the heads are confidently wrong on
  ModernBERT features. That explains v2's high first loss but not the
  collapse.
- The earlier ModernBERT-specific leads (no [CLS] attention sink,
  start-of-text per-word tokenization) are not needed to explain it.

### Long run: feature distillation over a text pool

Pure distillation (no task loss) from gliner2.5-base's final hidden states:

- **Loss:** MSE in the teacher's per-dimension z-space through a learnable
  768x768 projection. Word and schema-marker positions align one to one (the
  processors emit identical word and marker counts; 64,000 of 64,000 rows
  aligned). Markers and words are averaged separately and summed.
- **Projection:** ridge-fitted on 300 rows before training (R² 0.54), then
  trained at lr 1e-4.
- **Encoder:** AdamW lr 3e-5, warmup 10%, linear decay, 8,000 steps of 8 rows
  (1.5 h on the Studio).
- **Data:** the pilot rows plus 79,844 AG News and Banking77 train texts, each
  under a random 4–16 label classification schema drawn from 115 names
  (labels and entity types). No labels are used.

### Stage 2: entity schemas

Resumed from step 8,000 (encoder and projection) for 6,000 more steps on the
same text pool, with half the rows under a random 2–10 type entity schema with
no mentions (upstream's processor builds the `[E]` layout from the types
alone), drawn from 36 type names. Same loss and optimizer, with a fresh warmup.
The student's own task forward is skipped when the task weight is zero, which
halved the step time (0.35 s/step; stage 1 paid for it needlessly).

| Mean (in-domain / held-out) | Classification accuracy | NER F1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gliner2.5-base, released | 0.728 / 0.483 | 0.543 / 0.515 |
| stage 1, step 8,000 | 0.639 / 0.370 | 0.167 / 0.113 |
| stage 2, step 6,000 | 0.662 / 0.378 | 0.449 / 0.364 |

Per dataset: Banking77 0.610, AG News 0.714, CLINC150 0.450, SST-5 0.304,
typed-decisions 0.379; CrossNER AI 0.381, literature 0.436, music 0.530,
politics 0.499, science 0.360, MIT restaurant 0.450, MIT movie 0.233.

What this establishes, and what it does not:

- A raw ModernBERT-base encoder can take on GLiNER2.5's representation from
  unlabeled text alone, and the teacher's trained heads then work on it. The
  earlier collapse is an initialization problem, not a limit of ModernBERT.
- The student is not yet at the gate: classification is 7–10 points and NER
  7–15 points short, on 14,000 steps of AG News and Banking77 text.
- The heads here are the teacher's, read through a 768x768 projection; the
  student has not been trained on any task loss. The Antenna recipe becomes:
  (1) feature distillation over a large, diverse text pool with mixed
  schemas; (2) fold the projection into the heads (or keep it as a layer);
  (3) task distillation (soft targets from Decide and gliner2-large) on top.

Next steps:

1. A native Zig feature-distillation objective (teacher final states in, a
   fitted projection, z-space MSE over aligned word and marker positions), so
   stage (1) runs in the native trainer as ANTENNA.md intends. The teacher's
   states can be precomputed once per text and schema.
2. A larger and more diverse text pool (the current one is news and banking
   queries only; MIT movie, the most distant held-out set, is weakest at
   0.233), longer runs, and relation and structure schemas.
3. Then task distillation with soft targets, and the step 1 gate.

## Operational notes

- ModernBERT-base jobs need job budgets above the small-DeBERTa defaults:
  about 12 GiB backend (the largest batch needed 9.5 GiB resident at batch 4),
  6 GiB host (9 GiB to restore a checkpoint), and larger tape limits.
- Each run writes a 2.5 GB optimizer checkpoint plus a 0.6 GB export; the
  job's disk preflight reserves both.
- On a shared machine the live-memory admission keeps a 9.66 GB dynamic
  reserve; other training jobs blocked the laptop, so the pilot ran on the
  Studio.
